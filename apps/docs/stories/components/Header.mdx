# Header

The sticky bar at the top of a product: brand at the start, a nav landmark in the middle, actions at the end.

<Canvas of={HeaderStories.Default} />

## Usage notes

- Pass links (anchors or router links) as `nav` children. Mark the current page with `aria-current="page"`; the header styles it as selected.
- The nav is a landmark labeled "Main" by default. Change `navLabel` when a page has more than one nav.
- `end` is for what belongs to the session: a scheme switch, the signed-in person. Keep it to two or three items.
